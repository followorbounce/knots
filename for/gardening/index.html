Best knots for

Gardening

Tying plants to stakes, bundling canes and fixing nets. Knots that grip without slipping and are easy to cut away.

  • Binding knots

    Clove Hitch

    Two turns round a post with the second crossing over the first.

    ●○○ Easy
  • Binding knots

    Timber Hitch

    A noose that grips a log, a pole or a bundle: the end goes round the standing part and is twisted back round its own turn.

    ●○○ Easy
  • Binding knots

    Constrictor Knot

    A clove hitch with an extra tuck, which locks the two ends under the crossing turn.

    ●●○ Intermediate
  • Lashings

    Sheer Lashing

    Lashes two poles side by side, to lengthen a pole or to make sheer legs (an A-frame).

    ●●○ Intermediate
  • Lashings

    Square Lashing

    Lashes two poles that cross at right angles, for frames, tables, gates and trellises.

    ●●○ Intermediate
