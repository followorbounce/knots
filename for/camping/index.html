Best knots for

Camping

Tarps, guy lines, bundles and washing lines. Knots that are quick to tie with cold hands and quick to undo when you pack up.

  • Loops

    Bowline

    The classic fixed loop.

    ●○○ Easy
  • Binding knots

    Clove Hitch

    Two turns round a post with the second crossing over the first.

    ●○○ Easy
  • Hitches

    Cow Hitch

    A bight of rope dropped round a rail with both legs pulled through it.

    ●○○ Easy
  • Loops

    Double Overhand Noose

    A running loop held by a double overhand tied round the standing part.

    ●○○ Easy
  • Hitches

    Half Hitch

    The simplest hitch: the end goes round the standing part and across itself.

    ●○○ Easy
  • Stopper knots

    Overhand Knot

    The simplest knot there is, and the first move of many others.

    ●○○ Easy
  • Hitches

    Round Turn and Two Half Hitches

    A round turn on a ring or post takes the load while you finish with two half hitches round the standing part.

    ●○○ Easy
  • Bends

    Sheet Bend

    The everyday knot for joining two ropes, including ropes of different thickness.

    ●○○ Easy
  • Stopper knots

    Slipped Overhand Knot

    An overhand knot finished with a bight instead of the end, so one pull on the short end releases it.

    ●○○ Easy
  • Binding knots

    Timber Hitch

    A noose that grips a log, a pole or a bundle: the end goes round the standing part and is twisted back round its own turn.

    ●○○ Easy
  • Hitches

    Two Half Hitches

    A turn through a ring or round a post, then two half hitches round the standing part, both the same way.

    ●○○ Easy
  • Loops

    Englishman’s Loop

    A fixed loop made of two overhand knots, one in the end round the standing part and one in the standing part round the end.

    ●●○ Intermediate
  • Hitches

    Rolling Hitch

    A hitch that holds against a pull along a pole or another rope, not just across it.

    ●●○ Intermediate
