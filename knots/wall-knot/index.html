Stopper knots

Wall Knot

A crown upside down: each strand passes under the next and comes up through its bight, so the ends point up out of the top. A small stopper on its own and the base of larger strand knots.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Unlay the end of the rope a short way and spread the three strands.
  2. 2Take the first strand round under the next one, leaving a bight.
  3. 3Take the second strand under the third the same way.
  4. 4Take the third strand under the first and up through the first strand’s bight. The ends point upward.
  5. 5Work each strand snug, a little at a time.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • A small stopper at the end of a laid rope.
  • With a crown on top, the manrope knot; the base of the Matthew Walker and diamond knots.

Watch out

  • Whip or tape the ends, or finish with another knot, or it can work loose.

Untying

Pull each strand back down out of the bight it passes through.

Related knots
