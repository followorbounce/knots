Buntline Hitch

Two half hitches tied back toward the ring instead of away from it, so the second hitch is trapped inside the first. It holds even when the line flogs and shakes, but it jams.

Untying

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Pass the end through the ring from front to back.
  2. 2Bring it down behind the standing part, below where the knot will be.
  3. 3Bring it round to the front of the standing part.
  4. 4First half hitch: round the standing part and across over itself, working up toward the ring.
  5. 5Second half hitch above the first, nearer the ring. The end finishes pointing toward the ring.
  6. 6Push the knot up to the ring and pull the end hard to set it.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Tying a line to a ring, shackle or eye that will be shaken about, such as a flag halyard.
  • Slippery modern line where two half hitches would work loose.

Watch out

  • It can jam hard after a heavy load.
  • Snug it right up against the ring before loading.

Untying

Push the knot away from the ring to loosen it, then work the end back out.
