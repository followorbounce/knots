Figure-Eight Loop

Also called Figure-eight on a bight, Flemish loop

A Figure-Eight Knot tied in a doubled rope. It makes a strong fixed eye that is easy to check by eye, which is why climbers use it.

Knots used to hold people need training. Learn life-safety knots from a qualified instructor, and have a partner check every one before it is loaded.

Use it for

  • A loop to clip a carabiner into, at the end or in the middle of a rope.
  • Anchors and rescue systems.
  • Any loop that must not slip, where untying is less important.

Watch out

  • Check that every part runs doubled and parallel, with no crossed strands.
  • For tying into a harness, tie a single Figure-Eight and follow it back through: the threaded version.
  • It is hard to untie after a heavy load.

Untying

Bend the knot sharply back and forth, then push the top bight down to open it.
