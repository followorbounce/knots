Double Sheet Bend

A sheet bend with a second turn of the thinner rope round the bight. Use it when the two ropes differ a lot in size, or when the rope is slippery.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Make a bight in the thicker rope (yellow).
  2. 2Bring the thinner rope (blue) up through the bight from behind.
  3. 3Take it round behind both legs of the bight, then round a second time beside the first turn.
  4. 4Bring it across the front and tuck it under itself both times, not into the bight.
  5. 5Hold the bight and pull the blue standing part to set the knot.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Joining a thin line to a much thicker rope.
  • Joining slippery or stiff rope, where a plain sheet bend can work loose.

Watch out

  • The thicker or stiffer rope always makes the bight.
  • Both short ends should finish on the same side.

Untying

Push the bight of the thicker rope toward the knot to loosen the turns, then pull the thin rope out.
