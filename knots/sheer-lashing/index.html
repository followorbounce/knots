Sheer Lashing

Also called Shear lashing

Lashes two poles side by side, to lengthen a pole or to make sheer legs (an A-frame). Start with a clove hitch, wrap round both poles, tighten with frapping turns between them, finish with a clove hitch.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Tie a clove hitch round one pole.
  2. 2Wrap the rope round both poles, six or more turns, working along them. Keep the turns snug and side by side.
  3. 3Take two frapping turns between the poles, round the lashing turns, and pull them hard.
  4. 4Finish with a clove hitch round the other pole.
  5. 5Check that the poles cannot slide against each other.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Joining two poles to make a longer one.
  • The top of an A-frame or a pair of sheer legs.
  • Bundling stakes or canes.

Watch out

  • Pull each turn tight before the next.
  • For an A-frame, leave the turns a little loose so the legs can open.

Untying

Undo the finishing clove hitch and unwind.
