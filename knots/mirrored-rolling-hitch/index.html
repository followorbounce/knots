Mirrored Rolling Hitch

The rolling hitch tied as its mirror image, for a pull coming from the opposite side. The two crossing turns always go on the side the load pulls toward.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1The standing part hangs in front of the pole.
  2. 2Take the end over the pole and round, crossing the standing part on the side the load will pull toward.
  3. 3Make a second turn the same way beside the first, also crossing the standing part.
  4. 4Go round once more on the far side and tuck the end under the second crossing turn.
  5. 5Snug it up and load the standing part along the pole, toward the two turns.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Taking strain off a rope or tying to a pole when the pull runs the other way along it.

Watch out

  • Like the rolling hitch, it only grips in one direction. Check which way the load will pull before you start.

Untying

Take the load off and slide it back; the turns open up.
