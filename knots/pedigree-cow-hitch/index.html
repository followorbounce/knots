Pedigree Cow Hitch

A cow hitch made secure for a pull on one leg only: the free end is tucked back up between the legs and under the bight, so it cannot slide out.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Start as for a cow hitch: one leg hangs down behind the rail.
  2. 2Up the back of the rail, over the top towards you, down the front and back under the rail.
  3. 3Behind the rail it crosses behind both legs: this is the bight.
  4. 4Round the rail the other way and down the back between the rail and the bight; then tuck the end back up between the two legs, under the bight, and down outside it.
  5. 5Load the standing leg; the tuck stops the hitch slipping.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Hanging something from a rail or ring by one end of a rope.
  • Where a plain cow hitch keeps slipping.

Watch out

  • Leave a long tail so the tuck cannot pull out.

Untying

Pull the tucked end back down out from under the bight, then open the cow hitch.
