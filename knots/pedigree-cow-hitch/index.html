Pedigree Cow Hitch

A cow hitch made secure for a pull on one leg only: the free end is tucked back up under the bight, so it cannot slide out.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Start as for a cow hitch: one leg hangs below the rail.
  2. 2Under the rail to the front, up the front, over the top and down the back.
  3. 3Across the back, behind both legs, just under the rail: this is the bight.
  4. 4Round the rail on the other side and out under the bight, then tuck the end back up between the legs and the bight, and let it hang down behind it.
  5. 5Load the standing leg; the tuck stops the hitch slipping.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Hanging something from a rail or ring by one end of a rope.
  • Where a plain cow hitch keeps slipping.

Watch out

  • Leave a long tail so the tuck cannot pull out.

Untying

Pull the tucked end back down out from under the bight, then open the cow hitch.
