Two Half Hitches

A turn through a ring or round a post, then two half hitches round the standing part, both the same way. Together the half hitches form a clove hitch on the standing part.

Easy

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Pass the end through the ring from front to back.
  2. 2Bring it down behind the standing part.
  3. 3Bring it round to the front.
  4. 4First half hitch: round the standing part and across over itself.
  5. 5Second half hitch, the same way, below the first. Tuck the end under.
  6. 6Slide the hitches up to the ring and pull them snug.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Tying a line to a ring, a post or an eye.
  • Finishing a tie-down or a guy line.

Watch out

  • Make both half hitches in the same direction, or you get a cow hitch that slips.
  • For a load you need to ease off, add a round turn first.

Untying

Undo the two half hitches and pull the end back through the ring.
