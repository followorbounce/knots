Stopper knots

Slipped Overhand Knot

Also called Slip knot

An overhand knot finished with a bight instead of the end, so one pull on the short end releases it. A quick stopper you can take out again in a moment.

Difficulty
●○○ Easy
Security
Low
Untying
Easy

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Hold the standing part on the left. The working end does the moving.
  2. 2Bring the working end up and round to make a loop, crossing the standing part.
  3. 3Fold the end back on itself and push this bight, not the end, behind the standing part and up through the loop.
  4. 4Pull the bight and the standing part to tighten. Leave the short end free.
  5. 5To release, pull the short end.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • A temporary stopper you expect to remove.
  • Holding a coil, a bag neck or a bundle for a short while.
  • Anywhere a plain overhand would jam and you need it out again.

Watch out

  • A tug on the short end, or a snag, undoes it.
  • It will not hold against a heavy or jerky load.

Untying

Pull the short end. The bight slides out and the knot falls apart.
