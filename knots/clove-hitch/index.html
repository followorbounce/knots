Binding knots

Clove Hitch

Also called Builder’s knot

Two turns round a post with the second crossing over the first. It is quick to tie, easy to adjust, and it starts most lashings.

Use it for

  • A quick tie to a post, a stake or a rail.
  • Hanging fenders on a boat for a short time.
  • Starting and finishing square and diagonal lashings.

Watch out

  • It can work loose if the post turns or the pull keeps changing direction.
  • For anything that matters, add a half hitch round the standing part or use a Round Turn and Two Half Hitches.
  • It holds poorly on a square post or a flat surface.

Untying

Push the crossing turn back toward the standing part and it falls open.

Related knots

  • Constrictor Knot The same shape with one extra tuck. It grips hard and stays put.
  • Round Turn and Two Half Hitches More secure for a long-term tie.
  • Rolling Hitch Holds against a pull along the pole.
