Bowline with Stopper

A bowline with its tail tied in an overhand knot round the leg of the eye beside it, so the bowline cannot shake loose when it is not loaded.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Tie a bowline: the standing part runs up.
  2. 2Make the small loop.
  3. 3Form the eye.
  4. 4Up through the small loop.
  5. 5Round behind the standing part.
  6. 6Back down through the small loop.
  7. 7Take the tail round the leg of the eye beside it and tie an overhand knot.
  8. 8Pull the overhand snug up against the bowline.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Knots used to hold people need training. Learn life-safety knots from a qualified instructor, and have a partner check every one before it is loaded.

Use it for

  • A bowline that will be loaded and unloaded repeatedly.
  • Stiff or slippery rope, where a plain bowline can loosen.

Watch out

  • Tie the overhand snugly against the bowline, round the leg of the eye, not round the standing part.

Untying

Undo the overhand first, then break the back of the bowline.
