Stopper knots

Double Overhand Knot

Also called Barrel knot (two turns)

An overhand knot with one extra turn. It makes a rounder, bulkier stopper that will not pull through a hole an overhand slips through, and it grips hard.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Run the working end along beside the standing part.
  2. 2Turn the end back on itself.
  3. 3Wrap it twice round the standing part, working back along it.
  4. 4Tuck the end back through the inside of both wraps, so it comes out past the turn.
  5. 5Pull the end and the standing part so the wraps close up into a neat barrel.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • A stopper in thin cord, string or fishing line.
  • Finishing the tail of another knot so it cannot work back.
  • One half of the Double Fisherman’s Knot.

Watch out

  • It jams. Once loaded it can be very hard to untie.
  • Dress the turns so they lie side by side, not crossed.

Untying

Loosen it by pushing the turns apart with a spike, then work the end back out.
