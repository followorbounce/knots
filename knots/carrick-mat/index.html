Carrick Mat

The carrick bend’s weave closed into a flat oval and followed round twice. A small mat, coaster or base for a decoration, and the simplest of the woven mats.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Lay out the carrick pattern loosely on a table: loops crossing strictly over and under.
  2. 2Close the pattern: the end meets the start.
  3. 3Follow the pattern round a second time, beside the first pass.
  4. 4Complete the second pass and work out the slack so the mat lies flat. Tuck the ends underneath.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Coasters, small mats and pot stands.
  • A flat decoration on a bag, box or wall.
  • Learning the over-under logic of woven mats.

Watch out

  • Lay the first pass loosely; it is much easier to follow round a loose pattern.
  • Keep the parallel passes flat beside each other, not on top of each other.

Untying

Pull the end back out the way it went.
