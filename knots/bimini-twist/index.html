Bimini Twist

A long, strong doubled loop for fishing line. The loop is twisted many times, the end is wound back down over the twists, and a half hitch round one leg locks it. It keeps almost the full strength of the line.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Double the line and twist the loop round itself many times.
  2. 2Hold the loop open and let the end wind back down over the twists.
  3. 3Lock it with a half hitch round one leg of the loop.
  4. 4Pull the hitch snug and trim the tag.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Making a double line for the end of a fishing line.
  • A strong loop to tie a leader to.

Watch out

  • It takes practice: the twists must be wound back evenly, with the loop held open.

Untying

It is usually cut off.
