Four-Strand Round Sennit

Four strands braided into a firm round cord. Each strand goes round behind the others and comes forward between the two on the other side, so two strands spiral each way, over one and under one.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Hold four strands together at the top; take each in turn round behind and forward between the two opposite.
  2. 2Keep going, always with the strand that is highest: two spiral each way, over one and under one.
  3. 3Finish with a whipping or a crown knot.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Lanyards, key cords and bell ropes.
  • A rounder, stronger cord than a flat braid.

Watch out

  • Keep the tension even, or the cord will twist and kink.

Untying

Work the strands back out from the end.
