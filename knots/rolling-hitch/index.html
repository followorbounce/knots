Rolling Hitch

Also called Magnus hitch

A hitch that holds against a pull along a pole or another rope, not just across it. The two crossing turns go on the side the load will pull toward.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Taking the strain off a rope, for example to free a jammed sheet on a winch.
  • Tying to a pole or rail when the pull runs along it.
  • Adjustable tie-downs and guy lines.

Watch out

  • It only grips in one direction. Pulled the other way it slides.
  • Load the standing part along the pole toward the two turns.
  • On a slippery or very smooth spar, add an extra turn.

Untying

Take the load off, slide the knot back the other way, and the turns open up.

Related knots

  • Clove Hitch The same finish without the extra turn. It slips under a lengthwise pull.
