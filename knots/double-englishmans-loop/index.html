Double Englishman’s Loop

Also called Double fisherman’s loop

The Englishman’s Loop tied with double overhands. Very secure, very hard to untie.

Difficulty
●●○ Intermediate
Security
Very high
Untying
Very hard

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1The standing part runs in from the right.
  2. 2Near the loop, the standing part ties a double overhand knot round the end.
  3. 3The rope goes round to form the eye and comes back as the end, through that first knot.
  4. 4The end ties its own double overhand round the standing part.
  5. 5Pull so the two knots slide together and lock.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • A permanent loop in slippery line or cord.
  • A loop that must not slip under repeated loading.

Watch out

  • Treat it as permanent once loaded.
  • Dress the wraps neatly so both knots nest together.

Untying

Usually you cannot after a heavy load.
