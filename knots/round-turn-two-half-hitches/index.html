Round Turn and Two Half Hitches

A round turn on a ring or post takes the load while you finish with two half hitches round the standing part. It is secure, and it can be untied even with the rope under strain.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Pass the end through the ring from front to back.
  2. 2Pass it through again, so the rope makes a full round turn on the ring.
  3. 3Bring the end down behind the standing part and round to the front.
  4. 4First half hitch: take the end round the standing part and across over itself.
  5. 5Second half hitch, the same way, below the first. Tuck the end under.
  6. 6Slide the half hitches up toward the ring and pull them snug.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Mooring a boat to a ring or post.
  • Tying off a load, a tarp line or a hammock.
  • Any tie to a fixed object that you will want to undo later.

Watch out

  • Make both half hitches in the same direction. Together they form a clove hitch round the standing part.
  • Leave a tail of a few rope-widths after the second half hitch.

Untying

Undo the two half hitches. The round turn holds the load while you ease it off.

Related knots

  • Clove Hitch Quicker, but not as secure.
  • Bowline A loop dropped over a post does a similar job.
  • Fisherman’s Bend tucks the first half hitch through the round turn for even more grip.
