Eight-Strand Square Sennit

Eight strands braided into a thick cord with a square section, each strand passing over two and under two. Firm, chunky and decorative.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Hold eight strands together, four on each side; take each in turn round behind and forward, over two and under two.
  2. 2Work alternately from each side, pulling each strand firm.
  3. 3Finish with a whipping.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Heavy lanyards, handles and bell ropes.
  • Decorative work where a thick square cord looks right.

Watch out

  • Count carefully: one strand out of pattern shows along the whole length.

Untying

Work the strands back out from the end.
