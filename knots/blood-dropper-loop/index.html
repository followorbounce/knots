Blood Dropper Loop

A loop standing out from the middle of a line, at right angles to it. The two sides of a loop are twisted round each other, and the loop is pushed through the gap in the middle of the twists.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Make a loop in the line and twist the two sides round each other, several times each side of the loop.
  2. 2Open the middle of the twists and push the loop through the gap.
  3. 3Hold the loop and pull the two sides of the line apart; the twists close round it.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Attaching extra hooks or flies to a fishing line.

Watch out

  • Keep the same number of twists on each side.
  • Wet the line before tightening, and pull the two sides of the line apart slowly.

Untying

It is usually cut off rather than untied.
