Splices & whippings

Short Splice

Joins two three-strand ropes end to end: the ends are married so their strands interlock, then each rope’s strands are tucked into the other rope against the lay. Stronger than any bend.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Unlay both ends and push them together so each strand lies between two strands of the other rope.
  2. 2Tuck the yellow rope’s strands into the blue rope, against the lay, over one and under one.
  3. 3Turn the work round and tuck the blue strands into the yellow rope the same way.
  4. 4Make three rounds of tucks each side, roll the splice even and trim.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • A permanent join in three-strand rope.
  • Making an endless strop or grommet from one rope.

Watch out

  • The splice is about twice the rope’s thickness and will not run through a block made for the rope.
  • Use rope of the same size and lay on both sides.

Untying

It is permanent; cut it out if you must.
