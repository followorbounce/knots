Hitches

Highwayman’s Hitch

A quick-release hitch on a rail made only of bights: one bight round the rail, a second through it, and a third through the second. The standing part takes the load; one pull on the end and the hitch falls away.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1The standing part comes up in front of the rail, as a bight up through the first bight.
  2. 2The first bight goes over the rail from behind and hangs in front.
  3. 3A bight of the end goes up through the second bight.
  4. 4Pull the standing part to tighten. Pull the end to release.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • A quick tie to a rail that must come free at once.
  • Tying up a horse or a boat for a short time.

Watch out

  • It can shake loose without a load. Check which strand is which: pulling the standing part tightens it, pulling the end releases it.
