Square Lashing

Lashes two poles that cross at right angles, for frames, tables, gates and trellises. A clove hitch, three wrapping turns, two frapping turns and a finishing clove hitch.

LashingsCampingGardening
Difficulty
●●○ Intermediate
Security
High
Untying
Easy

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Tie a clove hitch on the upright, just below the crossbar.
  2. 2Wrap round the crossing three times: in front of the crossbar, behind the upright, in front, behind.
  3. 3Take two frapping turns between the poles, round the wrapping turns, and pull them tight.
  4. 4Finish with a clove hitch on the crossbar.
  5. 5Pull everything snug so the joint cannot twist.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Frames, shelves and gateways from poles.
  • Garden frames and trellis.
  • Camp gadgets and bridges.

Watch out

  • Each wrapping turn goes outside the last on the crossbar and inside it on the upright, so the turns lie neatly.
  • Pull every turn tight; a loose square lashing twists.

Untying

Undo the finishing clove hitch and unwind.
