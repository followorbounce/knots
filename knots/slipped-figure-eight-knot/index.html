Slipped Figure-Eight Knot

A figure-eight finished with a bight, so a pull on the short end releases it. A stopper that is quick to tie and quicker to take out.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Hold the standing part at the top. The working end hangs below.
  2. 2Bring the end up and across the standing part to make a loop.
  3. 3Carry it on round, behind the standing part.
  4. 4Fold the end into a bight and push the bight down through the first loop.
  5. 5Pull the bight and the standing part to tighten. A pull on the short end undoes it.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • A stopper that has to come out fast.
  • Holding a line temporarily, for example while you sort out a tangle.

Watch out

  • A snag on the short end can release it.
  • Draw it up firmly or it can shake loose.

Untying

Pull the short end.
