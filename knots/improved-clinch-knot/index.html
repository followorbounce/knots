Improved Clinch Knot

A clinch knot with one extra tuck: after going through the small loop by the eye, the end goes back through the big loop it has just made. The tuck stops the knot slipping in thin or slippery line.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Pass the end of the line through the eye of the hook.
  2. 2Bring it back up alongside the line.
  3. 3Twist the end round the line five times, working away from the hook.
  4. 4Bring the end back down and through the small loop just above the eye.
  5. 5Then tuck it back through the big loop you have just made.
  6. 6Wet it and pull the line steadily so the twists close onto the eye. Trim the end.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Tying thin or slippery line to a hook, lure or swivel.

Watch out

  • Wet the knot before tightening.
  • Tighten slowly so the twists lie neatly, without crossing.

Untying

Cut it off and tie again.
