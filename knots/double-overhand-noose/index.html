Double Overhand Noose

Also called Double overhand sliding loop, Strangle noose

A running loop held by a double overhand tied round the standing part. Pull the standing part and the loop closes; the knot grips well enough to stay where you leave it.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Make a loop and bring the end back alongside the standing part.
  2. 2Wrap the end twice round both parts, working toward the loop.
  3. 3Tuck the end back through the inside of both wraps.
  4. 4Pull the end to tighten the knot, then pull the standing part to close the loop.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Snugging a loop round a post, a bundle or a ring.
  • Starting a lashing or a tie-down.
  • Attaching line to a hook or ring.

Watch out

  • It is a noose: it tightens under load. Never put it round a person or an animal.

Untying

Open the loop wide, then loosen the wraps and push the end back out.
