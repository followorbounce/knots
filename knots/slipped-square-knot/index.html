Binding knots

Slipped Square Knot

Also called Slipped reef knot

A square knot with one end pulled through as a bight. It binds the same way, and a pull on the short end releases it at once.

Easy

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Make a bight in the left-hand end (yellow).
  2. 2Bring the blue end in from the right, over the curve of the yellow bight and under its lower leg.
  3. 3Take it round outside the neck of the yellow bight, over both yellow legs.
  4. 4Instead of pulling the end right through, fold it into a bight and bring the bight out under the upper leg and over the curve.
  5. 5Pull the bight and the standing parts to tighten. Leave the short end free for release.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Reefing a sail, where the knot must come undone quickly.
  • Tying sacks, bundles and covers you will open again soon.

Watch out

  • Not for joining ropes under load.
  • A snag on the short end releases it.

Untying

Pull the short end of the slipped part.
