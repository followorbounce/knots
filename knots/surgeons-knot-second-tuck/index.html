Surgeon’s Knot with Second Tuck

The surgeon’s knot with the extra twist in both halves. It is bulkier, but grips better still in slippery line.

●●○ Intermediate

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Cross the two ends and twist them round each other twice.
  2. 2Pull the double twist snug.
  3. 3Cross the ends the opposite way and twist them twice again.
  4. 4Pull all four ends to tighten.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Slippery or springy line where even the surgeon’s knot works loose.

Watch out

  • As with the square knot, the second half must go the opposite way to the first.
  • Not a bend for loads: use a proper bend to join ropes.

Untying

Loosen each double twist in turn.
