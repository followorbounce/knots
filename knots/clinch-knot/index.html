Clinch Knot

The classic way to tie line to a hook, lure or swivel: through the eye, five twists round the line, then back through the small loop by the eye.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Pass the end of the line through the eye of the hook.
  2. 2Bring it back up alongside the line.
  3. 3Twist the end round the line five times, working away from the hook.
  4. 4Bring the end back down and through the small loop just above the eye.
  5. 5Wet it, then pull the line so the twists slide down onto the eye. Trim the end.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Tying monofilament line to a hook, lure or swivel.

Watch out

  • Use five or six twists in normal line, fewer in very thick line.
  • Wet the knot before tightening it.
  • In slippery or braided line, use the Improved Clinch or a Palomar.

Untying

Usually you cut it off and tie again.
