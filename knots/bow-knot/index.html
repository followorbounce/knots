Binding knots

Bow Knot

Also called Shoelace knot, Double slipped square knot

The knot on your shoes: a square knot with both ends slipped. Pulled tight it lies flat with the two loops sideways; pull either short end and it comes apart.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Make the first loop in the left-hand end (yellow): a bight, with a short end left over.
  2. 2Bring the blue end over the base of that loop and under it.
  3. 3Take it round the neck of the yellow loop.
  4. 4Push a bight of the blue end through, under the upper leg and over the curve, to make the second loop.
  5. 5Pull both loops outward to tighten. They should lie flat and sideways.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Shoelaces and drawstrings.
  • Ribbons on gifts.
  • Anything tied often and undone often.

Watch out

  • If the loops sit up and down instead of sideways, it was tied as a granny and will come undone. Retie it the other way.
  • For slippery laces, take an extra turn in the first half.

Untying

Pull one of the short ends.
