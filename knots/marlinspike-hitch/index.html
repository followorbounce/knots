Marlinspike Hitch

A loop laid over the line with a spike or rod pushed through it: over the loop, under the line, over the loop. It gives you a handle to pull on a thin line, and vanishes the moment the spike is drawn out.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Hold the line taut. Make a small loop in it and lay the loop over the line below.
  2. 2Push the spike over the near side of the loop, under the line, and over the far side.
  3. 3Pull the line against the spike. To release, slide the spike out.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Pulling hard on thin line or twine, for example when tightening a whipping or seizing.
  • A quick handle on any line.

Watch out

  • It only holds while the line is under tension, and only in one direction.
  • Any smooth rod, pen or screwdriver will do instead of a spike.

Untying

Pull the spike out; the loop falls away.
