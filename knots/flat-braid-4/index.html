Four-Strand Flat Braid

Also called 4-strand plait

A flat braid of 4 strands. Alternately from the left and the right, the outermost strand is brought to the middle, passing over and under the strands on its way.

Security
Holds when the end is bound
Untying
Easy

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Bind the strands together at the top and spread them flat. Bring the outer left strand to the middle, over-under the strands it passes.
  2. 2Now the outer right strand to the middle, the same way. Then left again, then right.
  3. 3Carry on alternating. Pull each strand snug as it reaches the middle.
  4. 4Bind the strands together at the end.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Lanyards, straps, bracelets and decorative cords.
  • Hair, bread and rush work use the same pattern.

Watch out

  • Bind the strands together at the start, and again at the end, or the braid unravels.
  • Keep an even tension so the edges stay straight.

Untying

Unbind the end and unbraid it.
