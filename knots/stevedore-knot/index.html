Stopper knots

Stevedore Knot

A figure-eight with one more turn round the standing part. The result is a bigger stopper that is still easy to untie.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Hold the standing part at the top. The working end hangs below.
  2. 2Bring the end up and across the standing part to make a loop.
  3. 3Take it round behind the standing part...
  4. 4...and round once more, so it wraps the standing part twice.
  5. 5Bring the end back down through the first loop.
  6. 6Pull the end and the standing part to draw the knot up.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • A stopper that has to block a larger hole.
  • Rope ends in blocks and fairleads.
  • Stopping a rope pulling through a hand or a pulley when lifting.

Watch out

  • Dress the turns neatly before tightening or it will not form properly.

Untying

Push the bights apart, the same way as a figure-eight.
