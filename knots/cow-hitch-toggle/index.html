Cow Hitch with Toggle

A cow hitch through a ring where a wooden toggle takes the place of the ends: the bight is caught round the toggle. Pull the toggle out and the rope drops free.

Untying
Quick release

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Pass a bight of rope up through the ring from the front and down behind it.
  2. 2Bring the bight forward, outside the legs, and up in front.
  3. 3Push the toggle through the bight so the bight wraps round it.
  4. 4Pull the legs down to snug the bight onto the toggle.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • A quick-release tie to a ring or eye.
  • Where there is no access to the ends of the rope.

Watch out

  • Keep the toggle in place: if it can slide out by itself, so can the rope.

Untying

Pull out the toggle.
