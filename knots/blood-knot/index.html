Blood Knot

Also called Barrel knot (bend)

The angler’s join for two lines of similar size: each end twists round the other line about five times and comes back through the gap in the middle. Slim, smooth and strong.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Overlap the two ends.
  2. 2Wrap the yellow end round the blue line about five times.
  3. 3Bring it back and push it through the gap where the two lines cross.
  4. 4Wrap the blue end round the yellow line the same number of times, turning the other way.
  5. 5Bring it back through the same gap, from the opposite side.
  6. 6Wet the knot and pull the standing parts slowly until the wraps bed down. Trim the ends.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Joining two lengths of fishing line.
  • Building tapered leaders from lines of slightly different thickness.

Watch out

  • Wet the line before pulling it tight, or the heat of friction weakens it.
  • The two ends must go through the centre gap in opposite directions.
  • Not for lines of very different thickness.

Untying

You cannot, in practice. Cut it out and tie again.
