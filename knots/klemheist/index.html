Klemheist Knot

A friction hitch tied with a loop of cord or tape. The loop is wound up round the main rope and the bottom end is passed through the top bight. It grips when pulled down and slides when pushed up, and works with tape as well as cord.

  1. 1The main rope (blue) hangs straight down.
  2. 2Hold a loop of cord (yellow) against the main rope.
  3. 3Wind the loop up round the main rope, three or four turns, keeping the turns neat.
  4. 4Leave a short bight at the top of the turns.
  5. 5Pass the bottom end of the loop up through the top bight and pull it down.
  6. 6Tighten the turns and load the cord downward.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Knots used to hold people need training. Learn life-safety knots from a qualified instructor, and have a partner check every one before it is loaded.

Use it for

  • Climbing a fixed rope or holding a load on it.
  • A backup on a rope system when only tape is to hand.

Watch out

  • It grips in one direction only: the bight that the legs pass through must be at the top.
  • Use cord clearly thinner than the main rope, or the hitch will not grip.

Untying

Unload it, loosen the bight and unwind the turns.

Related knots
