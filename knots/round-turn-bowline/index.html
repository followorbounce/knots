Round Turn Bowline

Also called Double bowline

A bowline with the small loop taken twice round. The extra turn adds friction, so it holds better in stiff or slippery rope.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1The standing part runs up. Leave enough rope for the eye.
  2. 2Make a small loop, with the eye side crossing over the standing part...
  3. 3...and take it round a second time, beside the first, also over the standing part.
  4. 4Let the rope hang to form the eye and bring the end back up.
  5. 5Pass the end up through both turns of the small loop from behind.
  6. 6Take it round behind the standing part.
  7. 7Bring it back across the front and down through both turns, beside itself.
  8. 8Hold the end against the eye and pull the standing part to set the knot.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • A bowline in synthetic or slippery rope.
  • A loop that will be loaded and unloaded repeatedly.

Watch out

  • It can still shake loose when not loaded. Leave a long tail.
  • Both turns of the small loop must lie the same way.

Untying

Push the collar round the standing part away from the eye, as for a bowline.
