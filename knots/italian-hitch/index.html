Italian Hitch

A hitch on a carabiner or bar that lets rope run under control. Both strands leave on the same side of the bar, and the rope rubs on itself and the bar. It reverses neatly: pull the other strand and the hitch flips through the bar and runs the other way.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1The loaded strand comes up to the bar.
  2. 2Take it over the bar and round, as for a cow hitch.
  3. 3Bring it across the front, over one leg and under the other.
  4. 4Round the bar again: both strands now leave on the same side.
  5. 5Hold the free strand to control the rope as it runs round the bar.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Knots used to hold people need training. Learn life-safety knots from a qualified instructor, and have a partner check every one before it is loaded.

Use it for

  • Lowering or belaying with a carabiner when no belay device is available.
  • Controlling a load on a rail or bar.

Watch out

  • Use a pear-shaped locking carabiner, with the gate away from the running rope.
  • It twists the rope; that is normal.

Untying

Unload it and lift it off the bar.
