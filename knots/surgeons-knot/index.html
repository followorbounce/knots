Binding knots

Surgeon’s Knot

A square knot with an extra twist in the first half. The double twist grips while you tie the second half, so the knot does not loosen in between.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Cross the two ends and twist them round each other twice.
  2. 2Pull the double twist snug: it holds by itself.
  3. 3Cross the ends the opposite way and twist them once.
  4. 4Pull all four ends to tighten.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Tying a parcel or bundle when the first half keeps slipping.
  • Tying slippery line.

Watch out

  • It is still a binding knot: do not use it to join two ropes that carry a load.
  • Make the second half the opposite way to the first, as for a square knot.

Untying

Loosen the second half, then pull the double twist apart.
