Bowline

The classic fixed loop. It does not slip or jam under load, and it is easy to undo afterwards. The end ends up inside the loop, beside the leg of the eye.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • A loop at the end of a mooring line to drop over a post.
  • Tying a sheet to the clew of a sail.
  • A loop round a tree or a ring that you want to undo easily later.

Watch out

  • It can shake loose when it is not under load, especially in stiff or slippery rope.
  • Leave a long tail, or back it up with a stopper round the eye leg.
  • For climbing tie-ins use the Figure-Eight follow-through instead.

Untying

Push the collar round the standing part away from the eye ("break its back") and the knot loosens.
