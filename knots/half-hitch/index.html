Half Hitch

The simplest hitch: the end goes round the standing part and across itself. On its own it is not secure, but it is the building block of many hitches and the usual way to finish one.

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Pass the end through the ring from front to back.
  2. 2Bring it down behind the standing part.
  3. 3Bring it round to the front of the standing part.
  4. 4Take it round the standing part and across over itself.
  5. 5Pull the end snug against the standing part.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Finishing another knot, or backing one up.
  • A momentary hold while you tie something better.
  • Two of them in a row make the Two Half Hitches.

Watch out

  • A single half hitch can slip or spill. Do not trust it alone with a load.

Untying

Slack the standing part and pull the end back out.
