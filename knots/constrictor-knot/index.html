Binding knots

Constrictor Knot

A clove hitch with an extra tuck, which locks the two ends under the crossing turn. It grips a round object so hard that it often has to be cut off.

High
Untying
Hard

Drag to turn the knot. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

  1. 1Bring the working end across the front of the post.
  2. 2Take it round behind the post and back to the front, above the standing part.
  3. 3Cross it diagonally over the first turn and round behind the post again.
  4. 4Bring the end to the front, over the standing part and under the diagonal. The two ends now cross under it.
  5. 5Pull both ends hard. The diagonal clamps the crossing underneath it.

Arrow keys step through. Tags show whether the moving end passes over or under.

Use it for

  • Closing the neck of a sack or bag.
  • Holding a rope end temporarily before whipping it.
  • Clamping a hose, or holding parts together while glue sets.

Watch out

  • Hard to untie once tight. Tie it slipped if you will want it back.
  • It needs a round, firm object. On a flat face it loses its grip.
  • Cutting it off: slice across the diagonal turn on top, never toward yourself.

Untying

If it is loose enough, work the diagonal turn free with a spike. Otherwise cut the diagonal.
